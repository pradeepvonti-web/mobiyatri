Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 · This is a plain-language policy; a legal review is planned before public launch.

Who we are

MobiYatri ("we") provides travel eSIM data plans and related services to travellers, through mobiyatri.in and the MobiYatri apps.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and (if you sign in with Google) your Google profile name and email.
  • Order data: the eSIM plans you buy, prices paid, order references, and the eSIM identifiers (ICCID, activation codes) needed to deliver your service.
  • Support data: messages you send to our support assistant, plus basic device information (such as phone model) used to give accurate setup help.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) needed to run and secure the service.

We do not collect or store your card or UPI details — payments are processed by licensed payment providers.

How we use it

  • To deliver your eSIM (including sending the QR/activation code to your email).
  • To show your purchases in your account across devices.
  • To provide customer support, including AI-assisted support that references your orders.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

  • eSIM network providers — to provision your plan on their networks.
  • Payment providers — to process payments.
  • Infrastructure providers — database/auth (Supabase), email delivery (Resend), and AI support processing (Anthropic). Each receives only what is needed for its function.
  • Insurance partners — only if you choose to request travel-insurance cover; cover is provided by IRDAI-licensed insurers.

We do not sell your personal data.

Retention & your rights

We keep account and order data while your account is active and as required by law. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing hello@mobiyatri.in. Deleting your account removes your personal data except records we must keep for legal or tax reasons.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@mobiyatri.in.
