Stay on Wi-Fi

Install in one step

iPhone (iOS 17.4+): tap "Install in one tap" and the eSIM installer opens with your code pre-filled. Android: copy the code, then Settings → Connections → SIM manager → Add eSIM → enter manually.

Turn on when you land

At your destination, enable the MobiYatri line and switch on data roaming for that line only.
