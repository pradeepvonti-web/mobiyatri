Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Plain-language terms; a legal review is planned before public launch.

The service

MobiYatri sells prepaid travel eSIM data plans supplied by international network providers. Plans are data-only unless stated otherwise: they do not include a phone number, calls, or SMS.

Your responsibilities

  • Confirm your device is eSIM-compatible and network-unlocked before purchase (dial *#06# — an EID number means eSIM support). Purchases made for incompatible or locked devices are not refundable once the activation code has been issued.
  • Keep your activation QR/code private. One code installs on one device, once.
  • Use the service lawfully and in line with the local regulations of the country you visit.

Activation & validity

Plan validity starts when the eSIM first connects to a supported network in its coverage area — not at purchase or installation. Unused plans remain installable for the period stated at purchase. Data speeds depend on local networks and are not guaranteed.

Payments & refunds

Prices are shown in Indian Rupees and include applicable taxes unless stated. Because eSIM activation codes are digital goods delivered instantly, orders are generally non-refundable once the code is issued — except where the service could not be provided due to a fault on our or our network partners' side. Contact support and we will make it right where the fault is ours.

Travel insurance

Travel insurance shown in MobiYatri is offered by IRDAI-licensed insurers via referral partners. MobiYatri does not sell or underwrite insurance and is not an insurance intermediary; the insurance contract is between you and the insurer.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim related to a plan is limited to the amount you paid for that plan. We are not liable for losses caused by network outages, coverage gaps, device incompatibility, or events outside our reasonable control.
