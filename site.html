Questions, answered

What is an eSIM and will my phone support it?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone — no plastic card. iPhone XS/XR and newer, Pixel 3+, Samsung S20+ and most flagships after 2020 support it. Quick test: dial *#06# — if you see an EID number, you're good. Your phone must be network-unlocked.

When should I buy and install?

Any time before your trip. Install on home Wi-Fi before you fly — validity only starts when the eSIM first connects to a network at your destination, so buying early costs you nothing.

Will my Indian number still work?

Yes — your Jio/Airtel/Vi SIM stays in the phone for incoming SMS and OTPs. Just keep data roaming OFF on it, and use the MobiYatri line for data. WhatsApp keeps working with your Indian number.

How do I pay?

UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, and AMEX — all in ₹, no forex mark-up on your card.

What if something doesn't work?

Yatri Sahayak, our AI assistant, resolves most setup issues in seconds, 24/7, in English or हिन्दी. For anything it can't fix, human support is a WhatsApp message away.
